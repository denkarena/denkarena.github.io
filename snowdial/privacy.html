Privacy Policy for Snowdial

Last updated: 5 November 2026. The German version is legally binding.

Erdi Karasungur, Bieberer Str. 52, 63065 Offenbach am Main, Germany

2. Game data on your device

Snowdial needs no account. Your progress is stored on your device: solved levels, stars, coins, outfits, missions, streaks and settings. We do not receive this data. If Google device backup is turned on, Android may keep it in your personal backup with Google.

If the game crashes, it stores a technical crash report on the device (app version, device model, Android version, time, error trace). On the next start you can choose to share it yourself; otherwise it never leaves the device.

Streak reminders are optional. They are scheduled on your device only and can be turned off in the settings.

3. Online duels and leaderboards

Ranked duels and public leaderboards require an account with a confirmed email address. Friend duels by code work without email registration; a random online player ID is created for them. Sign-in and registration use an email code without a password. Offline play needs no account. There we store: a random player ID, the player name you chose, your outfit and title, your Brain Gym rating, your duels with solutions, points and times, your duel rating and season points, and season prizes you won. Other players can see your name, outfit, title, ratings and duel results. If you report a player, we store the report with your player ID.

The server runs at Supabase (Supabase Inc., USA), stored in the EU (Frankfurt). Supabase processes the data on our behalf; for technical reasons IP addresses appear in logs. Data may be transferred to the USA, safeguarded by EU standard contractual clauses. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, as duels are a feature you choose to use.

Name changes: For confirmed name changes we store the chosen name with a transaction identifier, the next permitted change time and the total coins spent on name changes. These records prevent duplicate charges and enforce the waiting period. They are not public and are removed when the online profile is deleted.

Account and progress: We store your confirmed email address for ranked online play and account backup. Your account also saves your game (levels, stars, coins, outfits, Sun Pass, missions, settings) so you can load it on another phone. We only send sign-in codes to the address, delivered by Resend (Resend Inc., USA, EU-US Data Privacy Framework). Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

We keep the data while you use the online features. You can delete it at any time in the app under Settings › Delete online data, or with an account under Account › Delete account (how to: Delete account), or by email to snowdial@denkarena.com.

3a. Optional gameplay statistics and verified rated puzzles

You can enable Settings › Share play statistics to share daily counts of sessions, puzzle starts and results, hints, ads and completed purchases. This is off by default and requires an existing online account. These statistics contain no move sequences, advertising IDs, location or payment details. They are linked to your player ID and are not anonymous. Administrators see aggregates with small groups suppressed.

The purpose is to improve onboarding, difficulty and game content. Consent remains on this device and does not transfer through cloud saves. Reports cover up to 90 days. Older daily records are removed on the next statistics request or administrator report. Turning the option off requests deletion and retries while the same account remains signed in. Signing out or changing accounts also attempts deletion. If the connection is unavailable, data may remain until regular cleanup. Deleting your account also removes its statistics.

For public rated-puzzle scores, the server checks the started puzzle, submitted move sequence and result. This online game feature is separate from optional statistics.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads via Google AdMob (Google Ireland Ltd., Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). Google may process your advertising ID, IP address, approximate location (derived from the IP address), device and app information and ad interactions to serve and measure ads, to personalise ads with your consent, and to prevent fraud.

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25(1) TDDDG), requested on first launch through Google's consent form. Without consent the app shows only non-personalised ads where permitted (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can change your choice at any time under Settings › Privacy options, and reset or delete the advertising ID in the Android settings.

Data may be transferred to the USA. Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. More: policies.google.com/privacy and policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

While Snowdial Plus is active, the app shows no automatic full-screen ads between levels. Previously purchased permanent ad-removal rights remain valid. You can still start optional reward videos yourself.

Optional crash reports

Crash reporting is off by default and separate from gameplay analytics. With consent and an existing online profile, the app sends app version, build, Android API and technical stack frames on a subsequent start. Reports exclude exception messages, email addresses, player names and device identifiers. The existing Supabase user ID is associated internally for rate limits, retry deduplication and account deletion; administrators see aggregate counts. Disabling clears unsent reports. Uploaded events are visible for up to 90 days and cleaned up on the next submission or admin retrieval; account deletion removes associated events. Up to five unsent reports remain locally until a successful retry. Manually sharing a detailed local crash report remains a separate player-initiated action.

5. Purchases and subscription (Google Play)

Payments for purchases and the "Snowdial Plus" subscription are handled by Google Play. We receive no card or bank details. For verification, the app sends the product ID and Google purchase receipt (purchase token) to our Snowdial server at Supabase; the server checks the purchase status and subscription validity directly with Google. This uses an existing online session or creates a random guest identifier. Purchases do not require email registration. The verification function keeps no separate receipt database and does not log purchase tokens. Unlocks and already processed receipts remain on your device and are included in your game backup if progress backup is enabled. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing your purchase). Google's privacy policy also applies.

6. Contacting us by email

When you write to us, for example via Settings › Contact support, your message is delivered through Cloudflare's forwarding service (Cloudflare Inc., USA) to our mailbox at Google (Google Ireland Ltd.) and stored there so we can reply. When you use the button in the app, your mail app adds the app version, device model, Android version, language and, if available, your player ID below your message. You can see and delete these details before sending. Both providers are certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (handling your request). We delete messages once they are resolved and no retention obligation applies.

7. Children

Snowdial is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR), and you may withdraw any consent with effect for the future. Write to snowdial@denkarena.com. For data held by Google, please contact Google directly. To delete your game data, clear the app's data in the Android settings or uninstall the app. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

9. Changes

We update this policy when Snowdial changes. The current version on this page applies.
