Wandelzug: every move transforms your piece

A puzzle you will only find in DenkArena. It looks like chess but plays completely differently.

Collect all the coins

Land on every coin square with your piece. The fewer moves you need, the more stars you get.

Your piece changes

After every move it becomes the next piece in the sequence, for example from rook to bishop and then to knight.

New every day

240 levels in twenty chapters, 96 of them free, and every day a puzzle that is the same for everyone. In the workshop you build your own levels and share them by code.

Along the way you meet portals, gates with keys, stamp squares and numbered coins.

The games

Chess

Ten strength levels from newcomer to grandmaster. Play the computer, a friend on the same phone or people online with a chess clock. Online games count towards your chess rating.

Game analysis

After every game you see where it turned and why another move would have been better. Then you practise your mistakes in the same position.

♟

Learn chess

13 short lessons: how each piece moves, how to capture, escape check and deliver mate. At the end you play your first game.

Chess puzzles

Over 800 checked mate puzzles from mate in 1 to mate in 3. A daily puzzle every day plus the three-minute sprint.

Sudoku

Minesweeper and Connect Four

Frequently asked questions

What does DenkArena cost?

The app is free and shows ads now and then, but never during a game. Premium costs €0.99 a month and can be cancelled monthly.

Do I need an account?

No. You can start playing right away. With a free account your progress is safe on a new phone too.

Is DenkArena available for iPhone?

At the moment DenkArena is only available for Android.

What data do you store?

Only what the app’s features need. All details are in the privacy policy.

How can I delete my account?

In the app under Settings, or on the delete account page.
